📖 What Is AmberText?

AmberText is a writing app built for people who want to focus on words instead of menus. It is a desktop program for Mac and Windows that lets you write in Markdown — a simple, plain-text way of formatting writing — while showing you a clean, finished-looking page right beside what you type. No programming knowledge is required to use it. If you can type in a document, you can use AmberText.

The idea behind AmberText is simple: your writing deserves a calm, uncluttered space. There are no pop-ups asking you to upgrade, no social feed, no notifications pulling at your attention. Just you, your words, and a quiet page that stays out of the way. Your notes are saved as ordinary text files on your own computer, so they are yours to keep, back up, move, or open with any other program.

AmberText is completely free and open source. That means anyone can inspect how it works, and it is not funded by advertising or by selling your data. It is funded by the simple fact that people want good tools to write with.

🖋️ Your First Five Minutes

When AmberText opens, you will see a writing area. That is all you need to get started. Here is a gentle path for the first few minutes:

  1. Start typing. Type a sentence, a heading, or the first line of a journal entry. Nothing is permanent until you decide it is.
  2. Watch the preview. As you write, a second pane shows how your page will look when formatted. Headings grow larger, lists get bullets, and bold text becomes bold.
  3. Add a little structure. Type a single # followed by a space to make a big heading. Type a dash and a space to make a bullet point. That is the whole secret to Markdown.
  4. Save your file. Give your document a name and choose a folder you will remember. AmberText saves your work as a plain text file, which stays readable even in other applications.
  5. Write without worrying. If you make a mistake, undo works exactly as you expect. Nothing is sent to a server, so nothing leaves your computer.

There is no wrong way to use AmberText. Some people keep one long running document. Others make a new file for every day, project, or idea. Both work perfectly well.

✨ What You Can Do With It

Live preview, side by side

Write on one side and see the finished page on the other. There is no "preview mode" to switch into and out of — the result of your formatting appears as you type, so what you see is what you get.

Diagrams with Mermaid

Need a flowchart, a timeline, or a simple process diagram? AmberText understands Mermaid, a plain-text way of describing diagrams. You write a few lines describing the boxes and arrows, and a proper diagram appears in your preview. It is a fast way to explain an idea without opening a drawing program.

Math with KaTeX

If you write about numbers, science, engineering, or teaching materials, you can include mathematical formulas that render beautifully. Equations look like they were typeset in a textbook rather than typed on a keyboard.

A private assistant, on your device

AmberText includes an AI helper that runs locally on your own computer. You might use it to rephrase an awkward sentence, suggest a heading, or summarise a long note. Because it works on-device, your text is not uploaded anywhere and does not pass through an outside service.

Everything else you would expect

A comfortable writing area, distraction-free formatting, fast search for your files, standard keyboard shortcuts, and plain text documents that never lock you in. Small conveniences that stay out of the way until you need them.

🔒 Private by Design

AmberText is local-first and offline-first. Both phrases mean the same practical thing for you: the app works fully without an internet connection, and your documents live on your hard drive rather than in someone else's data centre. There is no sign-up screen, no email address to hand over, and no sync account to manage.

This matters for diaries, therapy notes, client drafts, research notes, and anything else you would not want floating around online. When the AI features are used, the processing happens on your machine. Nothing is sent out.

If you want a backup, simply copy your folder of notes into your usual backup location. Because the files are plain text, they will still open in twenty years, in any editor, on any operating system.

📝 Markdown in Plain English

Markdown is a way of marking up a document using characters already on your keyboard. Instead of clicking bold buttons, you put two asterisks around a word. Instead of choosing a heading from a dropdown, you put a hash symbol at the start of a line. AmberText turns those marks into polished formatting in the preview.

  • Heading: a hash and a space at the start of a line.
  • Bold: two asterisks on each side of the word.
  • Italic: one asterisk or one underscore on each side.
  • List: a dash and a space at the start of each line.
  • Link: square brackets around the label, round brackets around the web address.

You do not have to memorise any of this on day one. AmberText keeps your text readable either way, so you can add formatting gradually as your confidence grows.
